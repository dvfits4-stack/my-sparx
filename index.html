🧠 GCSE Exam Checklist

  • Write the equation before calculating.
  • Convert units before substituting.
  • Show working and include units.
  • Use an appropriate number of significant figures.
  • For practicals: identify IV, DV and control variables.
  • For graphs: label axes with quantities and units.
  • For chemistry: check atoms on both sides when balancing.
